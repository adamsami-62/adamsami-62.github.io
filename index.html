NYC Crash Injury Analysis

Mapped 782K police-reported NYC crashes onto 262 neighbourhoods to show where, when and how badly crashes happen. A gradient-boosting model and a logistic regression, tested on a held-out year, show that what a crash involved explains injury risk far more than where or when it happened. The top-scored 10% of crashes involved an injury 85% of the time, against 44% overall.

Python GeoPandas Scikit-learn Statsmodels

OCT Scan Disease Detector

A convolutional neural network trained from scratch to classify retinal OCT scans as CNV, DME, drusen or normal. Reached 85.2% test accuracy against 75.5% for a HOG + SVM baseline, then used Grad-CAM to check which parts of each scan drove its decisions, and tested it on scans from a different scanner.

Python PyTorch Computer Vision Grad-CAM
